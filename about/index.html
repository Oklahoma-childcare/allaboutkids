THE CENTER AT A GLANCE

Growing up happens one day at a time.

All About Kids Discovery Learning Academy is listed as a child care center in Moore, serving infants, toddlers, preschoolers, and school-age children.

Oklahoma Human Services lists the program as year-round, with child care subsidy participation and weekday hours from 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
