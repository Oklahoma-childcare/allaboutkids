01What ages does All About Kids serve?+

Oklahoma Human Services lists care for infants (0–11 months), toddlers (12–23 months), preschoolers (24–48 months), and school-age children ages 5 and older. Call for room details and availability.

02What are the regular opening hours?+

The current state listing shows Monday through Friday, 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM, with Saturday and Sunday closed. Holiday schedules may differ.

03Where is the center located?+

The center is at 1050 S Santa Fe Ave, Moore, Oklahoma 73160. You can use the directions link on the Contact page.

04Does the center participate in child care subsidies?+

Yes. The Oklahoma Human Services listing indicates that the center accepts child care subsidies. Ask the team about eligibility and enrollment steps.

05Is the center licensed?+

The center is listed by Oklahoma Human Services under child care license K830058386 as a three-star program.

06What is the current tuition or weekly cost?+

Tuition rates are not confirmed in the available public records. Please call (405) 378-2977 for current prices, policies and payment details.

07Are there current openings for my child?+

The 160-child figure is licensed capacity, not availability. Ask the center directly whether it has openings for your child’s age group.

08How can I arrange a visit?+

Call (405) 378-2977 or email jdslearning30@yahoo.com to ask about visiting the center and learning more about its programs.
